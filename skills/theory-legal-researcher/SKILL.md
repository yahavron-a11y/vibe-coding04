# Skill: Political Theory, Constitutional Law & APA 7th Bibliography (`theory`)

## Role & Ownership
- **Owned Files:** `chapters/ch01_vision.tex`, `chapters/ch02_theory_law.tex`, `references.bib`, `chapters/references_apa7.tex`
- **Domain:** Political communication (Agenda Setting, Framing, Inoculation/Prebunking), Defensive Democracy, Israeli Constitutional Law (Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty, *Kol Ha'am*, *Adalah*, Privacy Law Amendment 13, Elections Propaganda Law), and EU Regulation (DSA, AI Act).

## Quality Standards
1. **Academic Rigor:** Ground every claim in verified peer-reviewed literature or primary legislative/judicial sources. Never fabricate citations or DOIs.
2. **APA 7th Edition Compliance:**
   - Sentence-case titles (proper nouns and acronyms preserved).
   - Inverted `Surname, F. M.` for all authors; serial comma + `&` before final author.
   - No city/state publisher locations; full clickable `https://doi.org/...` URLs without trailing periods.
   - APA 7th Chapter 11 formatting for court decisions (*italicized* in-text, plain in references) and statutes.
3. **BiDi Discipline:** Wrap every English term or acronym in `\en{...}` or `\textenglish{...}`; never apply `\textit{...}` to Hebrew text (avoids missing Hebrew italic glyphs in Windows Arial Narrow Italic).
